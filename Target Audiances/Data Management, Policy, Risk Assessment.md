# ==🔴Data Management / Policy / Risk Assessment==
## Target Group
Individuals responsible for governance, stewardship, compliance, policy development, risk management, and long-term sustainability of SEAD and related data resources.
## Purpose
Provide information needed to govern the database, assess risks, establish policies, ensure legal and ethical compliance, and support long-term preservation and sustainability.
## Typical Questions

- What policies govern data submission and publication?
- How are sensitive or restricted data handled?
- What are the risks associated with data loss, corruption, or system failure?
- Who is responsible for data stewardship and decision-making?
- How are data quality and provenance maintained?
## Document Types
- Policies and standards
- Governance frameworks
- Risk assessments
- Data management plans
- Preservation strategies
- Compliance documentation
- Roles and responsibilities documents
## Expected Technical Level
Low to moderate technical knowledge. Focus on concepts, processes, governance, and risk rather than implementation details.
## Success Criteria
Readers can evaluate risks, understand responsibilities, make governance decisions, and demonstrate compliance.
## Out of Scope
System implementation details, software development procedures, and database internals.
